# Further practice ideas

These are proposals, separate from the implemented activities.

- **Present Continuous — correct the reporter:** a picture appears with a false report, e.g. “She is sleeping.” The learner types “She isn't sleeping. She is reading.” Goal: negatives and contrast, beyond filling verb endings.
- **Near or Far — change viewpoint:** move the observer to the opposite side of a scene. The learner updates this/that/these/those while the objects remain fixed. Goal: understand that distance is relative to the speaker.
- **Four Baskets — listen and sort:** play a short instruction and select the matching object and basket. Goal: recognise singular/plural and near/far in speech. Keep an optional text transcript.
- **Prepositions — restore the room:** look at a prepared room, then put one moved object back according to one short instruction. Later, a partner describes the position without seeing the listener's room. Goal: comprehend and produce on, under, between, next to, in front of and behind.

Current implementation: 36 Present Continuous typewriter prompts; possession-pair backs without hints; snap animation and balanced sorting; five room types with two prepared arrangements each and three single-instruction tasks per room.
