# Weekly calendar: B1 and B2

Future practice now contains Printer, Weekly calendar, and Time lift.
The printer offers will, Present Simple timetables, and a mix of those two.
The calendar offers going to, Present Continuous arrangements, Future Continuous,
Future Perfect, Future Perfect Continuous, and Mix (all five represented in a round).
Old B1/B2 form links open the corresponding calendar focus. Lower-level going-to
practice retains its original interface. Old printer drafts remain in the previous
storage namespace, and calendar data uses new keys.

Each level has 14 activities over Monday–Sunday with start/end times. Event height
matches duration. All seven days fit beside the task at wide desktop widths; smaller
screens have a horizontal schedule scroller with task below it.
B1 uses Oliver and B1 Course vocabulary; B2 uses Amelia and approved B2 ALH words.
Colours use LevelDesign: B1 #001166/#f0f0e7; B2 #A9C632/#1D2E1B/#C8D2A6/#FFC4C5.

With questions: seven guided prompts with a future reference point, Hint and examples.
Free practice: no automatic initial selection. Learner clicks any activity, which gets
an animated circle, box or bracket. No question is shown. Free Mix shows examples of
all five perspectives. Responses are open; there is no automatic grammar grading.

«Зачеркнуть» draws through the event name; repeat removes it. No Cancelled status.
Positive and negative notes/reveals are saved separately, keyed by level, mode, focus
and event. Strikes persist across focuses within the same level. Reduced-motion and
shared replay suppress annotation animation. Navigation disposes annotations/observers.

Data: future-calendar-data.js. UI: future-calendar.js/css.
Tests: scripts/check-future-calendar.py and scripts/check-future-time.py.
Prepared and checked in draft; no publication in this task.
