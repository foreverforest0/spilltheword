## Grammar content — ongoing preference

Use grammatically and stylistically natural, original English at every level.
Correctness and natural phrasing take priority over inserting a course word.
Use actual B1 Course vocabulary at B1 and the user's corrected
`docs/b2-alh-approved.json` at B2; use that approved pool for C1 unless a separate
C1 list is supplied. Spread contexts across films, trends, social media, friends,
family, leisure, everyday decisions and work. Avoid repetitive office-only banks.
Model answers must reflect real grammatical alternatives and context-dependent
choices; do not reject an unchanged tense merely because backshift is possible.
Every activity must remain usable orally. In reported-speech badges the front
contains the original portrait, identity and quote only; nuances belong on the
back at B2/C1, without invented addressee/time story prompts. Preserve the exact
reference portraits and badge styling.
