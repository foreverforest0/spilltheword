# Sentence Expander — последовательности по всем темам

В каждой цепочке пять изменений. Примеры ниже — проверяемые модели; в игре принимаются сокращения и указанные британские/американские варианты.

## KIDS · To be

Начало: **Mia is happy.**

1. Add a colour: her bag is blue. Join with and.
   - Mia is happy and her bag is blue.
2. Change happy to sad. Keep the bag.
   - Mia is sad and her bag is blue.
3. Say she is not sad. Keep the bag.
   - Mia is not sad and her bag is blue.
4. Now ask only about Mia: is she sad? Use her name.
   - Is Mia sad?
5. Answer no, using she.
   - No, she is not.

## KIDS · Present Simple

Начало: **I play.**

1. Add what: football.
   - I play football.
2. Add when: every day.
   - I play football every day.
3. Change I to Tom.
   - Tom plays football every day.
4. Say Tom does not do this.
   - Tom does not play football every day.
5. Ask if Tom does this. Use a positive question.
   - Does Tom play football every day?

## KIDS · Present Continuous

Начало: **She is reading.**

1. Add what: a book.
   - She is reading a book.
2. Add where: at home.
   - She is reading a book at home.
3. Add when: now.
   - She is reading a book at home now.
4. Make it negative.
   - She is not reading a book at home now.
5. Ask if she is doing this. Use a positive question.
   - Is she reading a book at home now?

## KIDS · There is / are

Начало: **There is a cat.**

1. Add where: on the bed.
   - There is a cat on the bed.

   - There are not any cats on the bed.
4. Ask if there are any cats there.
   - Are there any cats on the bed?
5. Answer no.
   - No, there are not.

## KIDS · Whose / this / that

Начало: **This is a bag.**

1. Say it belongs to Mia: replace a with her.
   - This is her bag.
2. Replace her bag with hers.
   - This is hers.
3. Move it far away: change This to That.
   - That is hers.
4. Now there are two bags far away. Change That and is.
   - Those are hers.
5. Ask if those bags are hers. Keep the pronoun hers.
   - Are those hers?

## B2 · Third conditional

Начало: **Ben missed the train.**

1. Add the real result: so he arrived late.
   - Ben missed the train, so he arrived late.
2. Imagine the opposite past. Start If Ben had not missed the train; use would not have for the result.
   - If Ben had not missed the train, he would not have arrived late.
3. Replace would not have arrived late with would have arrived on time.
   - If Ben had not missed the train, he would have arrived on time.
4. Add the consequence: and he would have seen Mia.
   - If Ben had not missed the train, he would have arrived on time and he would have seen Mia.
5. Put the two result clauses first and the if-clause last.
   - Ben would have arrived on time and he would have seen Mia if he had not missed the train.

## B2 · Mixed conditionals

Начало: **Ben did not study medicine.**

1. Add a result that is true now: so he is not a doctor now.
   - Ben did not study medicine, so he is not a doctor now.
2. Imagine the opposite. Start If Ben had studied medicine; use would be a doctor now.
   - If Ben had studied medicine, he would be a doctor now.
3. Add a second present result: and he would work in a hospital.
   - If Ben had studied medicine, he would be a doctor now and he would work in a hospital.
4. Keep only the first result and put it first.
   - Ben would be a doctor now if he had studied medicine.
5. Change to a different mixed pattern: Ben is afraid of flying, so he did not travel yesterday. Start If Ben were not afraid of flying.
   - If Ben were not afraid of flying, he would have travelled yesterday.

## B2 · Wish / If only

Начало: **I do not have a car.**

1. Express a wish about now. Start I wish.
   - I wish I had a car.
2. Add the reason for the wish: so I could drive to work.
   - I wish I had a car so I could drive to work.
3. Now regret a past event: I did not buy the car. Use I wish and leave out the reason.
   - I wish I had bought the car.
4. Make that past regret stronger. Replace I wish with If only.
   - If only I had bought the car.
5. Now complain about an ongoing problem: the neighbour keeps blocking my drive. Use I wish and would stop.
   - I wish the neighbour would stop blocking my drive.

2. Say the repair is now complete. Use Present Perfect Passive and add in three months to show the time taken.
   - The bridge has been repaired in three months.
3. Add a deadline and move the completed repair into the future: by Friday. Remove in three months.
   - The bridge will have been repaired by Friday.
4. Change this into an obligation to finish before Friday. Use must, not Future Perfect.
   - The bridge must be repaired before Friday.
5. Make that obligation negative: say repairing it before Friday is forbidden.
   - The bridge must not be repaired before Friday.

## B2 · Reported speech

Начало: **Mia asked Ben, "Where do you live?"**

1. Report the question.
   - Mia asked Ben where he lived.
2. Change the original question to "Do you live in London?" and report it using if.
   - Mia asked Ben if he lived in London.
3. Change the original question to "Have you finished the report?" Report it using whether.
   - Mia asked Ben whether he had finished the report.
4. Now report a command: "Finish the report." Keep Mia and Ben.
   - Mia told Ben to finish the report.
5. Make the original command negative: "Do not finish the report." Report it.
   - Mia told Ben not to finish the report.

## B2 · Past modals of deduction

Начало: **Ben missed the train.**

1. Make it a strong deduction about the past. Use must have.
   - Ben must have missed the train.
2. Add the evidence: because he arrived late.
   - Ben must have missed the train because he arrived late.
3. Make the deduction uncertain. Use might have; leave out the evidence.
   - Ben might have missed the train.
4. Now say missing the train was impossible. Use cannot have.
   - Ben cannot have missed the train.
5. Give evidence for that impossibility: because I saw him on it.
   - Ben cannot have missed the train because I saw him on it.

## B2 · Relative clauses

Начало: **Mia is a doctor.**

1. Add extra information: she lives next door. Use a non-defining relative clause after Mia.
   - Mia, who lives next door, is a doctor.
2. Add information about her son instead. Replace the who-clause with whose son studies in Rome.
   - Mia, whose son studies in Rome, is a doctor.
3. Make only the relative clause negative.
   - Mia, whose son does not study in Rome, is a doctor.
4. Replace that relative clause with a reduced passive clause: Mia was trained in London.
   - Mia, trained in London, is a doctor.
5. Restore a full relative clause. Use who and Past Simple Passive.
   - Mia, who was trained in London, is a doctor.

## B2 · Gerund & infinitive

Начало: **Ben stopped smoking.**

1. Add when: last year.
   - Ben stopped smoking last year.
2. Change the meaning: during a walk, Ben paused in order to smoke. Use stopped to smoke; replace last year with during his walk.
   - Ben stopped to smoke during his walk.
3. Say he did not pause to smoke.
   - Ben did not stop to smoke during his walk.
4. Now say he remembered a task: taking his medicine before the walk. Use remembered to take and before his walk.
   - Ben remembered to take his medicine before his walk.
5. Change the meaning to a memory of a completed action. Replace to take with taking.
   - Ben remembered taking his medicine before his walk.

## B2 · Future Perfect / Continuous

Начало: **Mia will write the report.**

1. Show the action in progress at a future moment: at ten tomorrow.
   - Mia will be writing the report at ten tomorrow.
2. Ask a positive yes/no question about that future activity.
   - Will Mia be writing the report at ten tomorrow?
3. Now state that the report will be finished by Friday. Use Future Perfect; remove the clock time.
   - Mia will have written the report by Friday.
4. Make the completed-by-Friday statement negative.
   - Mia will not have written the report by Friday.
5. Ask what Mia will have written by Friday. Use a positive question.
   - What will Mia have written by Friday?

## B2 · Causative have / get

Начало: **A mechanic repaired Mia's car.**

1. Start with Mia and use had ... repaired to show someone did the work for her.
   - Mia had her car repaired.
2. Add when: last week.
   - Mia had her car repaired last week.
3. Make it a future arrangement: use is having and tomorrow instead.
   - Mia is having her car repaired tomorrow.
4. Say that arrangement is not happening.
   - Mia is not having her car repaired tomorrow.
5. Ask a positive question about the arrangement. Replace having with getting.
   - Is Mia getting her car repaired tomorrow?

## B2 · Comparison structures

Начало: **This room is bigger than that room.**

1. Add a large difference: far.
   - This room is far bigger than that room.
2. Express the opposite comparison. Start That room and use not nearly as big as.
   - That room is not nearly as big as this room.
3. Now say this room is exactly double the size. Start This room and use twice as big as.
   - This room is twice as big as that room.
4. Compare changes over time: this room becomes brighter when it becomes bigger. Use The bigger ... the brighter.
   - The bigger this room gets, the brighter it becomes.
5. Replace brighter with more expensive and keep the paired comparison.
   - The bigger this room gets, the more expensive it becomes.

## B2 · Collocations

Начало: **Mia makes a decision.**

1. Add when: every morning.
   - Mia makes a decision every morning.
2. Change to yesterday.
   - Mia made a decision yesterday.
3. Add a second action: and she took a break.
   - Mia made a decision yesterday and she took a break.
4. Add what she did next: after she did her homework. Put this clause at the beginning.
   - After she did her homework, Mia made a decision yesterday and she took a break.
5. Replace the time clause with Having done her homework. Leave out yesterday and the repeated she after and.
   - Having done her homework, Mia made a decision and took a break.

## B2 · Word formation

Начало: **It is possible to finish the report.**

1. Make possible negative using a prefix.
   - It is impossible to finish the report.
2. Add a reason: because the instructions are unclear.
   - It is impossible to finish the report because the instructions are unclear.
3. Express the same idea using the noun possibility. Start There is no.
   - There is no possibility of finishing the report because the instructions are unclear.
4. Make it positive. Use a possibility and clear instead.
   - There is a possibility of finishing the report because the instructions are clear.
5. Use an adverb from success instead. Start We can finish the report ... because the instructions are clear.
   - We can finish the report successfully because the instructions are clear.

## C1 · Inversion

Начало: **Mia has seen such a view.**

1. Make the statement negative using never.
   - Mia has never seen such a view.
2. Emphasise never by putting it first.
   - Never has Mia seen such a view.
3. Replace such a view with such a beautiful view before.
   - Never has Mia seen such a beautiful view before.
4. Now use this past sequence: Mia arrived, then the rain began immediately. Start No sooner and use than.
   - No sooner had Mia arrived than the rain began.
5. Express the same sequence with Hardly and when.
   - Hardly had Mia arrived when the rain began.

## C1 · Cleft sentences

Начало: **Mia sent Ben the report yesterday.**

1. Emphasise the person who sent it. Start It was Mia who.
   - It was Mia who sent Ben the report yesterday.
2. Emphasise the recipient instead. Start It was Ben whom Mia.
   - It was Ben whom Mia sent the report to yesterday.
3. Emphasise the time instead. Start It was yesterday that.
   - It was yesterday that Mia sent Ben the report.
4. Emphasise the action using a pseudo-cleft. Start What Mia did yesterday was.
   - What Mia did yesterday was send Ben the report.
5. Add exclusivity. Replace What with All and keep the rest.
   - All Mia did yesterday was send Ben the report.

## C1 · It’s time / would rather / subjunctive

Начало: **Ben should leave.**

1. Express that it is time for this to happen. Start It is time.
   - It is time Ben left.
2. Make it more urgent. Use high time.
   - It is high time Ben left.
3. Now express my preference instead: I would rather Ben left.
   - I would rather Ben left.
4. Say I prefer him not to leave.
   - I would rather Ben did not leave.
5. Now make a formal recommendation that he remain here instead. Start I recommend that Ben.
   - I recommend that Ben remain here.

## C1 · Participle clauses

Начало: **Mia finished the report.**

1. Add the later action: and then she went home.
   - Mia finished the report and then she went home.
2. Replace the first clause with a perfect participle clause.
   - Having finished the report, Mia went home.
3. Add a reason for leaving: because she felt tired.
   - Having finished the report, Mia went home because she felt tired.
4. Reduce the reason clause too. Use feeling tired after and, following Having finished the report.
   - Having finished the report and feeling tired, Mia went home.
5. Express the first action as incomplete instead. Use Not having finished the report and change went home to stayed at work. Keep feeling tired.
   - Not having finished the report and feeling tired, Mia stayed at work.

## C1 · Perfect infinitive & gerund

Начало: **Mia claims that she saw Ben.**

1. Replace that she saw with a perfect infinitive.
   - Mia claims to have seen Ben.
2. Add when she saw him: yesterday.
   - Mia claims to have seen Ben yesterday.
3. Make the claimed sighting negative, keeping claims positive.
   - Mia claims not to have seen Ben yesterday.
4. Express an admission instead: Mia admits that she did not see Ben yesterday. Use a perfect gerund.
   - Mia admits not having seen Ben yesterday.
5. Now make the admission positive and add a place: at the station. Put it before yesterday.
   - Mia admits having seen Ben at the station yesterday.

## C1 · Reported speech

Начало: **Mia said, "I have been waiting."**

1. Report the statement with that.
   - Mia said that she had been waiting.
2. Add a duration from her original words: for two hours.
   - Mia said that she had been waiting for two hours.
3. Add her next words: "I will not wait any longer." Join the reported clauses with and.
   - Mia said that she had been waiting for two hours and she would not wait any longer.
4. Replace said with told and add Ben as the listener.
   - Mia told Ben that she had been waiting for two hours and she would not wait any longer.
5. Now report Mia's question to Ben: "Why did you not call me?" Use asked, and leave out the previous statements.
   - Mia asked Ben why he had not called her.
