Original wobbly pencil artwork

These are the existing pictures from bcdf2f0, not newly drawn replacements.
78 primary pictures and 29 matching pencil-style action pictures are exported
as RGBA PNG files. RGB values and pencil texture are preserved. Transparent
safety margins prevent card edges from clipping drawings. For illustrations
with a paper background, only edge-connected paper alpha is cleared; enclosed
light details and warm skin colours are protected. Complete room scenes retain
their original background and all contextual details.

Five activities from the smooth alternate series use their existing matching
wobbly illustration. Seven unmatched smooth pictures are excluded from the
random action pool; the primary wobbly action series remains available.
manifest.json lists the source revision, PNGs, action subjects and exclusions.
The reproducible asset preparation is scripts/restore-wobbly-art.py.
