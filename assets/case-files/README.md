# Story files keepsakes

`porto-papers.png`, `london-papers.png`, and `bristol-papers.png` were generated for this activity on 11 November 2026. Each contains six recreated scanned-style objects: transport ticket, receipt, museum ticket, concert ticket, second receipt and blank clipped memo. They are educational illustrations, not real bookings or transactions. Native print supplies dates and prices without worksheet paragraphs, real payment codes or institution logos.

PNG sources are unchanged. Each SVG viewport in `present-perfect-files-data.js` uses source pixel coordinates, leaving natural paper edges intact. Source size: 1536 × 1024 per sheet, RGBA. The original generation files remain in `/workspace/generated_images`.

`photographs.png` is the existing six-frame generated photo sheet. Frames in reading order: Porto market camera, museum night, cassette repair, seaside wedding, museum café, family cassette. They illustrate original fictional vignettes, not documented events.

`paper-grain.svg` is the existing local procedural paper texture.

`porto-extras.png`, `london-extras.png` and `bristol-extras.png` add three generated 1254 × 1254 RGBA sheets, each with a souvenir attraction ticket and three isolated belongings. Attractions: Anne Frank House (Amsterdam, 6 September), LEGOLAND Windsor (23 August), Disneyland Paris (12 September). These tickets are earlier-trip keepsakes; their cities and dates are intentional. They are recreated souvenirs, not official admissions. Each PNG is unchanged from generation; SVG viewports select the objects.

Loose belongings: wired headphones, blue spiral notebook, sweet wrapper; sunglasses, folded map, mint tin; cassette tape, green notebook, tea packet. Handwritten reminder text is accessible HTML over the previously blank memo artwork and moves with it.
